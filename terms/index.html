Terms of sale

01Your order

When you place an order on this site we hold your date and send it to our kitchen. Your order is confirmed once we email you, within one working day. If we cannot make it, for example because the date is full, we tell you in that email and nothing is charged.

Cakes need at least 3 days' notice, and three-tier cakes six weeks. The designer only offers dates we can meet.

02Prices and payment

Prices are in US dollars and include the sponge, fillings and finish you choose. The price shown when you order is the price you pay. Payment is taken by card when you collect your cake or on delivery. Delivery is $18 within Nassau County.

03Handmade cakes

The drawing in the designer shows your choices; it is a guide, not a photograph. Every cake is piped and decorated by hand, so colours, flower placement and lettering will vary slightly from the drawing and from our photos.

Fresh flowers depend on the season. If a variety is not available we use a similar flower in the same colour and let you know beforehand.

04Allergies and dietary needs

Our kitchen uses wheat, dairy, eggs, tree nuts and peanuts. We take care, but we cannot promise any cake is free from these. Please tell us about allergies in the notes when you order, and talk to us before ordering for anyone with a severe allergy.

05Decorations that are not edible

Fresh flowers, candles, ribbons, cake boards and the supports inside tiered cakes are not edible. Remove them before cutting and serving. Edible gold leaf is safe to eat.

06Delivery, collection and changes

How delivery and collection work is set out in our delivery and collection policy. Changes, cancellations and refunds are covered in our refunds and cancellations policy. Once a cake has been collected or handed over, it is in your care.

07Photos of your cake

We sometimes photograph cakes for our website and social media. We never show your name or personal message without asking. If you would rather we did not photograph your cake at all, add a note to your order.

08The legal part

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of New York. Nothing in them limits your rights as a consumer under law. We may update these terms; the version on this page when you place your order is the one that applies to it.
