Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to make, deliver and confirm your cake, and we never sell it.

01What we collect

When you order, we ask for what we need to make and deliver your cake:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your delivery address, if you choose delivery.
  • Your cake choices, the message to pipe on top, any gift card note, and allergy notes you give us.
  • The date and window you choose.
  • Your email address, if you join our newsletter.

If you email, text or call us, we also keep what you send so we can answer it.

02How we use it

  • To make your cake and deliver it, or hand it over when you collect.
  • To confirm your order and contact you about it, for example if a flower is out of season.
  • To keep the records a business needs for accounting and tax.

03Who sees it

Only the people at Maison Sable who make and deliver your order, and the services that help us run the shop, such as our card payment provider and email service. They may use your information only to provide that service to us.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

04Our newsletter

If you join our newsletter, we use your email address to send news about seasonal cakes and offers, a few times a season. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us to be removed. Ordering a cake does not sign you up.

05What is stored on your device

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To make ordering easier, your browser keeps two things on your own device:

  • An unsent order you are still designing, for as long as the tab stays open.
  • Orders you place, so you can follow them on the tracking page from the same browser.

You can remove both at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

06How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for business accounts, usually seven years, and then delete them. Messages that are not part of an order are deleted after one year.

07Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we do not need to keep it by law. Email hello@maisonsable.example and we will reply within 30 days.

08Keeping it safe

The site is served over an encrypted connection. We never see or store your full card number: card payments are handled by our payment provider when you collect your cake or on delivery.

09Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Still have a question?

We answer within one working day.
