Delivery and collection

How your cake gets from our kitchen to your table, whether we bring it to you or you collect it from Franklin Square.

  1. 01Where we deliver
  2. 02Delivery windows
  3. 03Receiving your cake
  4. 04Tiered and wedding cakes

01Where we deliver

We deliver anywhere in Nassau County for a flat $18. For an address outside Nassau County, ask us first; we can often arrange it for an extra charge.

Every cake travels in our own chilled van, boxed and secured, driven by someone from the bakery.

02Delivery windows

You choose a two-hour window when you order:

  • 10 am – 12 pm
  • 1 pm – 3 pm
  • 4 pm – 6 pm

On Sundays the last window is 1 pm to 3 pm. We do not deliver or open on Mondays.

03Receiving your cake

Someone needs to be at the address to receive the cake. Our driver calls the phone number on your order as they arrive. If nobody answers, they wait ten minutes and try again.

If we still cannot hand the cake over, it comes back to the shop and you can collect it the same day. A second delivery is charged at the normal fee.

04Tiered and wedding cakes

Three-tier cakes are delivered fully assembled and set up at your venue by our team, included in the delivery fee within Nassau County. Please tell us the venue's access details and the name of a contact person on the day.

05Collecting from the shop

Collection is free. Come to the shop in Franklin Square during the window you chose and give your order number at the counter.

  • Carry the box flat, never tilted. The car floor is steadier than a seat.
  • Keep the car cool, with the air conditioning on in warm weather.
  • Drive gently: buttercream softens in the heat and tiers can shift on sharp turns.

06Once it's home

Keep the cake in the fridge in its box and take it out about an hour before serving, so the sponge and buttercream soften. Fresh flowers, candles and supports are not edible: remove them before cutting. Cakes are best eaten within three days.
