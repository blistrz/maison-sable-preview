Refunds and cancellations

Plans change. Here is how to change or cancel an order, and what we do if a cake is not right.

  1. 01Changing your order
  2. 02Cancelling a cake
  3. 03If something is not right
  4. 04What we cannot refund
  5. 05If we have to cancel
  6. 06How to reach us

01Changing your order

You can change the flavour, finish, message, date or delivery details for free up to 3 days before your date. Email us with your order number and what you would like to change.

Closer to the date we will still try. Once your sponges are baked we may not be able to change the flavour, but the message and delivery details can usually be updated until the day before.

02Cancelling a cake

Each cake is made only for you, so the charge depends on how close to your date you cancel:

  • More than 3 days before: free, nothing to pay.
  • 1 to 3 days before: 50% of the order, for ingredients bought and work started.
  • On the day: the full price.

For three-tier and wedding cakes, cancelling within four weeks of the date is charged at 50%, and within one week at the full price, because flowers and supplies are ordered well in advance.

03If something is not right

We check every cake before it leaves us. If something is wrong with yours, tell us within 24 hours of collection or delivery, with photos and your order number. Please keep the cake, or what is left of it.

If we made a mistake, for example the wrong flavour or message, or damage in our van, we will put it right with a replacement, store credit or a refund, whichever suits you.

04What we cannot refund

  • Damage after collection, during your own transport or storage.
  • Small differences from the drawing in the designer: every cake is decorated by hand.
  • Seasonal flower substitutions in a similar colour, which we tell you about in advance.
  • Personal taste, once most of the cake has been eaten.

05If we have to cancel

In rare cases, such as severe weather or an emergency in the kitchen, we may not be able to make or deliver your cake. We will contact you as early as we can to arrange a new date or give you a full refund of anything you have paid.

06How to reach us

Email hello@maisonsable.example or use the contact page. Have your order number ready (it looks like MS-1234). Refunds go back to the card you paid with and usually appear within 5 to 10 working days.
